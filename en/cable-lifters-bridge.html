Cables off the floor.

Every cable picks up interference where it touches floors and furniture. Bridge lifters keep that contact to a minimum: the cable rests in the notch and stays raised.

They are made of finger-jointed solid beech, treated with an oil that nourishes the wood and has antistatic properties. They also work well with shielded cables.

You'll find it in our showroom in Cesena. Fabrizio also builds it new, to the size of your equipment and your home.
