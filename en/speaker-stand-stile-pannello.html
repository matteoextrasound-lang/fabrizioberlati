A stand that is also an acoustic panel.

The stands echo the shapes of the wooden panels designed to tame specific frequencies: the slats protrude at different lengths and the acoustic correction happens on two opposite sides.

They are made of finger-jointed solid beech, left natural with no varnish or wood stain. They also come in fir and can be made in other woods.

You'll find it in our showroom in Cesena. Fabrizio also builds it new, to the size of your equipment and your home.
