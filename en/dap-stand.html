Your DAP becomes a desktop source.

Two 14 mm boards of oak-veneered plywood: one holds the player at an angle, the other is the base. The shaped base lets the 4.4 or 3.5 mm headphone connectors through and, with the iBasso DX270 and DX340, the external power supply too.

It's handy with headphones and IEMs, because the cable falls naturally, and when the player is connected to the hi-fi system as a source.

You'll find it in our showroom in Cesena. Fabrizio also builds it new, to the size of your equipment and your home.
