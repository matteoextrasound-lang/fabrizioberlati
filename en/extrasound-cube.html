How it's made.

Every Cube starts as five 18 mm plywood panels, with the layers of wood left visible on the edges. Plywood stays stable even with humidity, so the modules can sit side by side and on top of each other and stay square.

The proportions come from records and sleeves: the open structure keeps the sleeves in view and the records within reach while you listen. It also holds books, magazines and audio accessories.

  • The recesses for joining modules and the handles are cut into the panels: the module becomes a crate you can carry.
  • The Extrasound logo is engraved on the bottom.

It's a finished model: you'll find it ready in our showroom in Cesena.
