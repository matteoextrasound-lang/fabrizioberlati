Stripes of wood, columns of metal.

The top and base are in stained plywood. The stripes you see are the plywood's layers left on show: light and dark alternate and give the wood a fine, regular pattern.

Between top and base there are four black-painted metal columns, one at each corner. The stand looks light, almost transparent, and leaves the scene to the speaker.

The top measures 24.5 × 25 cm and is also the stand's whole footprint: it takes wide bookshelf speakers too. At 54 cm tall, the tweeter ends up around 75-80 cm from the floor, suited to seating a little lower than usual. For your own height there is the calculator.

You'll find it in our showroom in Cesena. Fabrizio also builds it new, to the size of your equipment and your home.

Frequently asked questions.

What seating height does it suit?

At 54 cm tall the Line puts the tweeter of a bookshelf speaker around 75-80 cm from the floor: it suits seating a little lower than usual. For your own setup there is the height calculator.

Will my speaker fit?

The top measures 24.5 × 25 cm and takes most bookshelf speakers, wide ones too; the speaker can also overhang by 2-3 cm. Above you will find a few examples.

Can it be made in other sizes?

Yes: I can change the height of the columns, the size of the top or the stain of the wood. See how made to measure.
